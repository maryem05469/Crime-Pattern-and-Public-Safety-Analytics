# Crime Pattern and Public Safety Analytics

## 1. Industry, Organization Description & Business Problem

### Industry: Public Safety & Urban Analytics
Public safety agencies such as city police departments, community safety offices, and urban policy teams analyze incident records to understand safety trends, allocate patrol resources, and improve response efficiency. Cities like Chicago publish open datasets to promote transparency and support data-driven decision making.

### Organization: Chicago Public Safety Analytics Office (CPSAO)
The Chicago Public Safety Analytics Office (CPSAO) is a specialized analytical unit that supports the Chicago Police Department and the City of Chicago by analyzing historical incident data to identify:
- When and where incidents most frequently occur  
- Community areas with emerging safety concerns  
- Incident types that are increasing or decreasing over time  
- The influence of temporal and environmental factors on public safety  

The organization develops Business Intelligence dashboards to assist decision-makers in planning patrol routes, improving resource allocation, and designing preventive strategies.

### Business Problem
Despite having access to millions of incident records, the city faces several challenges:
- Difficulty identifying spatial and temporal patterns in incidents  
- Limited visibility into districts requiring increased patrol presence  
- Insufficient understanding of seasonal variations and long-term trends  
- Lack of consolidated KPIs to evaluate public safety performance  
- Inefficient allocation of resources (patrols, community officers)  
- Difficulty communicating data-driven insights to policymakers  

### Goal
Develop a Business Intelligence solution that provides city officials with a clear and interactive view of incident patterns over time, across districts, neighborhoods, and incident types.  
The dashboard will help decision-makers detect anomalies, identify high-risk areas, and support strategic public safety planning.

## 2. Analytical Questions

### Temporal Trends
- How do incident counts vary by month, week, and time of day?  
- Are there seasonal patterns or recurring peaks throughout the year?  

### Geographical Analysis
- Which districts, wards, or community areas record the highest incident levels?  
- Are there zones showing consistent improvement or deterioration over the year?  

### Incident Characteristics
- What are the most frequent primary types of incidents?  
- How do incident trends differ across location types (street, residence, business, etc.)?  

### Operational & Resource Planning
- Which areas require increased patrol presence based on peak incident hours?  
- What is the distribution of incidents between weekdays vs weekends and daytime vs nighttime?  

### Case Outcomes
- What percentage of incidents result in an arrest, and how does this vary by district or incident type?  
- How long does it typically take for an incident record to be updated?  

### Predictive / Strategic
- Are there community areas experiencing emerging patterns that may require preventive measures?

## 3. Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)

### Core Volume KPIs
- **Total Incident Count**  
  Total number of reported incidents during the selected period.

- **Incidents per District**  
  Ranking of districts by total incident volume.

- **Incidents per Community Area / Ward**  
  Geographic distribution of incidents.

### Temporal KPIs
- **Monthly Incident Trend (%)**  
  Month-over-month percentage change in incident count.

- **Peak Incident Hour**  
  Hour of the day with the highest average number of incidents.

- **Incident Seasonality Index**  
  Variation of incident volume across seasons (winter, spring, summer, fall).

### Operational KPIs
- **Arrest Rate (%)**  
  Number of incidents resulting in arrest divided by total incidents.

- **Location-Type Distribution**  
  Percentage of incidents by location type (street, residence, business, etc.).

### Data Quality KPI
- **Data Update Lag (Days)**  
  Average time difference between the incident date and the record update date.
